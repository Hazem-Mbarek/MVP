CMR Convention — Article 9

Article
1. The consignment note shall be prima facie evidence of the making of the contract of
carriage, the conditions of the contract and the receipt of the goods by the carrier.
2. If the consignment note contains no specific reservations by the carrier, it shall be
presumed, unless the contrary is proved, that the goods and their packaging appeared to be in good condition when the carrier took them over and that the number of pack￾ages, their marks and numbers corresponded with the statements in the consignment note.
