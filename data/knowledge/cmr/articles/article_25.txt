CMR Convention — Article 25

Article
1. In case of damage, the carrier shall be liable for the amount by which the goods
have diminished in value, calculated by reference to the value of the goods fixed in accordance with article 23, paragraphs 1, 2 and 4.
2. The compensation may not, however, exceed:
(a) If the whole consignment has been damaged, the amount payable in the case of
total loss;
(b) If part only of the consignment has been damaged, the amount payable in the case
of loss of the part affected.
