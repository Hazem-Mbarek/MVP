CMR Convention — Article 26

Article
1. The sender may, against payment of a surcharge to be agreed upon, fix the amount
of a special interest in delivery in the case of loss or damage or of the agreed time-limit being exceeded, by entering such amount in the consignment note.
2. If a declaration of a special interest in delivery has been made, compensation for
the additional loss or damage proved may be claimed, up to the total amount of the interest declared, independently of the compensation provided for in articles 23, and 25.
