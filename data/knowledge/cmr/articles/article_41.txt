CMR Convention — Article 41

Article
1. Subject to the provisions of article 40, any stipulation which would directly or indirectly
derogate from the provisions of this Convention shall be null and void. The nullity of such a stipulation shall not involve the nullity of the other provisions of the contract.
2. In particular, a benefit of insurance in favour of the carrier or any other similar clause,
or any clause shifting the burden of proof shall be null and void.
